Custom Visualization

GramWay

GramWay is a galaxy of top 50 influenceers where each star represents a famous crator(pun intended!).
The visualization employs key MARKS including central stars (circles) for individual influencers, orbiting shapes (squares for posts, triangles for average likes, diamonds for engagement rates) for metrics, and orbital paths showing metric trajectories. Visual CHANNELS encode data through star size (follower count), star color (influence score from blue to red, ranging 70-93) and orbital element sizes (magnitude of respective metrics). INTERACTIVE features enhance exploration through hover effects revealing detailed metrics, animated orbits with continuous rotation of engagement metrics, and a force-directed layout where stars repel each other to maintain a clear galaxy formation.
This is a combinatorial visualization as in my opinion it amalgamates two known patterns: solar system/astronomy like vis(central body with orbiting elements) and force-directed network layout (for star positioning). I aimed at a space like diagram where each influencer has their own solar system with them being the STAR!(centre piece) while their planets are their account metrics, which acatually support them in becoming a star by gravitating around them! All together they make up a galaxy.
